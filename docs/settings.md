# Settings

Open Settings with the ⚙ in the status bar, by clicking your own name, from the palette, or `/settings`. Tabs are listed on the left.

## Account

- **Banner**: change or remove. For tall images, use the position slider to pick the crop, then **Save banner**.
- **Avatar**: change or remove.
- **Display name**: Save is enabled once you change it. Also `/nick <name>`.
- **User ID** with a copy button.
- **Password**: current, new, confirm. *Change password* enables when the new passwords match.
- **Log out** is at the bottom of the tab list.

## Preferences

**Timestamps**
- Hidden / short (14:05) / with seconds (14:05:09).
- 12-hour clock.

**Timeline**
- **Compact layout**: tighter rows, no avatars.
- **Group consecutive messages**: fade the name and hide the avatar on follow-ups.
- **Big emoji** for emoji-only messages.
- **Link previews**.
- **Autoplay GIFs**: off shows a still frame.
- **Expand code blocks** by default.

**Show in the timeline**
- Joins and leaves.
- Name and avatar changes.
- Room changes (name, topic, icon, permissions).
- Deleted messages: a "Message deleted" placeholder.
- Read receipts.
- Typing indicators.

**Composer**
- **Send with Ctrl+Enter**: Enter then adds a new line.
- **Markdown** formatting.
- **Emoji suggestions** for `:shortcodes:`.
- **Replace emoticons** with emoji.

**Privacy**
- **Send read receipts**: off sends private receipts, so your unread state still syncs but others can't see what you've read.
- **Send typing notifications**.
- **Share presence**.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: 9 built-ins plus your own (see [Themes](themes.md)).
- **Message font** and **sidebar font** (any installed font).
- **Message text size** (10–24px).
- A live preview.

## Notifications

Desktop alerts for this device and account-wide rules. See [Notifications](notifications.md).

## Voice & Video

Microphone, speaker, mic test, and audio processing. See [Voice and video](calls.md).

## Sessions

Every device signed in to your account:
- verified state;
- device ID;
- last activity and IP.

**Verify** an unverified session, or **Sign out** one (your password is required).

## Security

- **Session verification** status.
- **Key backup**: whether a server backup exists and whether this device uses it. **Enable backup** creates one and shows a **recovery key**: save it somewhere safe.
- **Recover from backup**: enter your recovery key or passphrase to unlock old encrypted messages.
- **Reset identity**: for when the recovery key is lost (password required). This replaces your backup; old messages not on this device stay unreadable, and other devices must verify again.

See [Encryption](encryption.md).
